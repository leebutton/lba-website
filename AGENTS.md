# Project working conventions

## Copy style

- Write in British English, using forms such as `organisational`, `mobilisation`, and `neighbourhood`.
- Use the Oxford (serial) comma consistently in lists of three or more items, including visible copy, metadata, alternative text, and accessibility labels.
- Preserve client testimonials verbatim. Do not change wording or punctuation inside a quotation unless Lee supplies or approves the correction.
- Prefer clear, specific language over generic consultancy terminology, and do not invent client outcomes, metrics, credentials, or experience.

## Brand and company details

- Use `Lee Button Associates` as the brand name.
- Use `Lee Button Associates Limited` wherever the legal company name is required, including copyright and structured company data.
