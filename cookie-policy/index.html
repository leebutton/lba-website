2. Our approach

We aim to collect as little information as possible when you use the website. We do not use website analytics, advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, behavioural profiling, or social-media tracking pixels.

3. Cookies and browser storage

The website does not currently use cookies, local browser storage, or similar technologies for its content or navigation.

Because the website does not set non-essential cookies or use equivalent technologies, it does not display a cookie-consent banner. We will review this position if the website’s technology changes.

4. Newsletter sign-up

Our newsletter sign-up sends the email address and associated technical information you submit to Buttondown only when you choose to subscribe. The form is ordinary website HTML: it does not load a Buttondown iframe or tracking script merely because you visit the page.

After you submit the form, Buttondown handles the confirmation and subscription-management process. Buttondown’s own privacy and cookie information applies when you visit pages it operates.

Newsletter open tracking and link tracking are disabled. Further information about newsletter data appears in our Privacy Policy.

5. External links and media

Links to external websites do not place those services’ cookies on this website merely because the links are present. A destination website may use its own cookies or related technologies after you follow a link.

If we introduce embedded YouTube videos or other third-party media, we will review the effect before publication. Where appropriate, we will prevent the third-party content from loading until you choose to use it and explain what will happen before it loads.

6. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if the technologies or providers used by the website change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
