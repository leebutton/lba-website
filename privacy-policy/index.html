3. The information we use and why

Enquiries and prospective work

We may use your name, job title, organisation, contact details, and information you include in correspondence with us. We use this to respond, understand your enquiry, arrange conversations, prepare proposals, and decide whether we can work together.

We normally rely on our legitimate interests in managing and developing our business. Where you ask us to take steps before entering into a contract with you personally, we may rely on those pre-contract steps.

We usually receive this information directly from you. We may also receive relevant business contact information from a colleague, mutual contact, public professional profile, or your organisation’s website.

You do not have to provide information when making an enquiry, but we may be unable to respond or explore working together without enough information to understand and answer you.

Client, supplier, and professional relationships

We may use names, job titles, business contact details, correspondence, meeting notes, contractual information, and payment and invoicing records. We use this information to provide and administer our services, manage suppliers and associates, maintain business and financial records, protect our legal rights, and meet legal, regulatory, tax, and accounting obligations.

Depending on the circumstances, we rely on performance of a contract, steps taken before entering into a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, or our legitimate interests in running the business and maintaining appropriate records.

Some information may be needed to enter into or perform a contract, or to meet a legal obligation. If it is not provided, we may be unable to begin or continue the relevant relationship.

Newsletter subscriptions

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we use your email address and the information needed to record and manage your subscription. Buttondown, our newsletter provider, may also process the IP address, referral information, and time associated with your subscription.

We use this information to confirm your subscription and send the newsletter you requested. We rely on your consent. Buttondown uses double opt-in, so you must confirm your email address before becoming an active subscriber.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any newsletter or contacting us. We do not use open-tracking pixels or tracked newsletter links.

Subscribing is optional. Choosing not to subscribe does not affect your ability to use the website or contact us.

Website hosting

This website is currently hosted using GitHub Pages. When you request a page, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, browser information, the requested page, and the date and time of the request to deliver and secure the service.

We do not receive visitor-level analytics from GitHub Pages and do not use this technical information to profile you. We rely on our legitimate interests in providing a secure and reliable website.

4. Sensitive information

If a client project requires us to process identifiable personal information, including health information, we will agree the roles, instructions, safeguards, and documentation needed for that project before processing begins.

Contact us if you would like more information about the safeguards applying to a particular transfer.

7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose for which it was collected, including legal, regulatory, accounting, and reporting requirements.

  • Enquiries that do not become client work are normally kept for up to 24 months after the last meaningful contact.
  • Client, supplier, and associate relationship records are normally kept for six years after the relationship or relevant transaction ends.
  • Accounting and tax records are kept for the period required by applicable law.
  • Newsletter subscription information is kept while you remain subscribed.
  • After you unsubscribe, we may retain a minimal suppression record for as long as needed to respect your request.
  • Information used to handle a request, complaint, or legal claim is kept for as long as needed to resolve it and protect the relevant legal rights.

8. Security

We use proportionate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We limit access to people and providers who need it for an authorised purpose and require appropriate confidentiality and security arrangements.

No internet or email service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If we become aware of a personal-data breach, we will assess it and make any notifications required by law.
